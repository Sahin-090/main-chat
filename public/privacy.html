Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026
Cute is a real-time chat application built around a temporary, privacy-first data model. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain and accurate terms, what information Cute collects, how it is used and stored, how long it is kept, and the choices available to you. We describe our actual technical implementation here — we do not make claims about protections that are not really in place.

1.Information We Collect

To create and operate your account, Cute collects:

  • Username — a unique identifier you choose to sign in with.
  • Password — never stored in plain text. Your password is hashed using the industry-standard bcrypt algorithm before it is saved; the original password is never retained.
  • Display name (optional) — shown to other users instead of your username.
  • Avatar (optional) — a colour swatch or an image you choose to upload.
  • Account role, online/last-seen status, and notification preferences — needed to run the real-time chat and notification features.

We intentionally ask for the minimum information required to run a messaging account — we do not require your real name, phone number, or email address to register.

2.Messages, Photos, Audio & Video

Cute lets you send text messages, photos, voice/audio messages, video, and other file attachments in one-to-one and group chats in real time. To deliver these features we store:

  • Message text content, timestamps, edit history flags, and delivery/read receipts.
  • Uploaded media files (images, audio, video, and other attachments), saved under a randomly generated, non-guessable filename rather than your original filename.
  • Metadata needed to route messages correctly — sender, recipient/group, chat type, reply references, and file size/type.

This content is transmitted between your device and our servers over an encrypted connection (see Section 5) and is stored so that the recipient(s) can retrieve it, and so that features like "delete for me" / "delete for everyone", edit history, and message search can work.

3.Information We Don't Intentionally Collect

Cute does not ask for or intentionally collect:

  • Your real name, government ID, or physical address.
  • Phone number or email address at registration.
  • Precise device location (GPS).
  • Contacts, photos, or files on your device beyond what you deliberately choose to upload.
  • Payment or financial information (Cute does not currently process payments).

4.How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Deliver real-time messages, media, presence status, and notifications.
  • Operate core chat features (search, reply, edit, delete, read receipts, groups).
  • Detect and prevent abuse, spam, and security threats.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot the service.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your message content to serve advertising.

5.Encryption & Security

We want to be precise here rather than make broad marketing claims. Cute's current, actual security implementation includes:

  • Encryption in transit: all traffic between your device and our servers — including chat messages sent over the real-time connection — travels over HTTPS/TLS and secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • Password hashing: passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage; Cute itself never has access to your plain-text password.
  • Access controls: authentication tokens and session checks gate access to your messages and account data; standard web-application protections (rate limiting, CSRF protection, and security headers) are applied to our API.
  • Media storage: uploaded files are saved under randomly generated filenames rather than their original names, reducing the chance of a file being guessed or discovered.
On "end-to-end encryption": message and media content is currently encrypted in transit (HTTPS/WSS) but is not end-to-end encrypted at the application layer — meaning message content is stored on our servers in a form our infrastructure can process (for example, to support search, edit, and delivery features), rather than being unreadable to the server itself. If and when true end-to-end encryption of message content is implemented, this policy will be updated to reflect it. We will not describe the service as "100% secure," "unhackable," or guaranteed leak-proof — no online service can honestly make that promise, and Cute doesn't either.

6.Temporary Data & 30-Day Retention

Cute is designed as a temporary chat service, not a permanent archive. The service's data-retention target is:

  • Messages are intended to be automatically and permanently deleted approximately 30 days after they are sent.
  • Photos, audio, video, and other attached files are intended to follow the same 30-day lifecycle and be permanently removed from storage, not merely hidden from view.
  • You can also manually delete a message ("delete for me" or "delete for everyone") at any time, independent of the 30-day cycle.
Please note: automatic, scheduled 30-day deletion is the retention policy Cute is built around, but it is an operational process that is still being rolled out on the backend. Until it is fully active, please do not treat the 30-day window as an absolute technical guarantee — if you need something gone immediately, use the in-app delete option, and avoid sending anything in chat that you would not want retained longer than expected. We will update this section once automatic expiry is fully enforced.

Some data falls outside the 30-day chat-content policy and is handled separately — see Sections 9 (device/IP logging) and 12 (backups) below. We do not claim that literally everything is deleted if security, abuse-prevention, or legally required records must be kept for a period.

7.Account Lifecycle

Your account itself is separate from your messages: your account (username, password hash, profile info) persists for as long as your account remains active. Account expiration, message expiration, media expiration, and security/operational logs are each governed by their own rules described in this policy — we do not lump them together.

8.Cookies & Local Storage

Cute uses cookies and your browser's local storage for functional purposes only — not advertising or third-party tracking:

  • Session/authentication cookies and tokens, used to keep you signed in and to protect state-changing requests (CSRF protection).
  • Local storage entries for your signed-in session details and your light/dark theme preference, kept on your own device.

9.Device, Browser & IP Logging

When you use Cute, our servers technically receive your IP address and basic request/browser information as part of normal web traffic, and this may be recorded in operational activity logs (for example, alongside actions like login or file upload) for security, abuse-prevention, and troubleshooting purposes. If you enable push notifications, we also store the browser-provided push subscription details needed to deliver them to your device. These operational/security logs are handled separately from the 30-day chat-content retention described in Section 6, and may be kept for a longer operational period where reasonably necessary for security and abuse prevention.

10.Third-Party Services

Cute relies on a small number of third-party infrastructure providers to operate:

  • Hosting & database providers, who run the servers and database that store your account, messages, and media.
  • Push notification delivery, via your browser/OS's push service, used only to deliver notifications you've opted into.
  • AI Assistant feature: if you use Cute's built-in AI Assistant, the message you send it and the limited application context needed to answer you are relayed through a proxy service to a third-party AI model provider in order to generate a response. Ordinary chat messages between users are not sent to this AI provider unless you specifically use the AI Assistant feature.

These providers process data only as needed to perform their function for Cute and are not authorised to use it for their own independent purposes.

11.Data Access, Sharing & Legal Requests

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Limited internal access to account and message data is restricted to what is necessary for operating and securing the service (for example, investigating abuse reports). We may disclose information where required by applicable law, a valid legal request, or where necessary to investigate suspected abuse, fraud, or a security incident affecting the service or its users.

12.Backups & Disaster Recovery

Cute does not currently maintain a separate, long-term backup archive of chat content beyond what our hosting/database provider may retain briefly for its own operational disaster-recovery purposes. If that changes, backup copies will be subject to their own expiration schedule, and this policy will be updated to describe it accurately.

13.Security Incident Handling

If we become aware of a security incident that affects your account or message data, we will take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, and remediate it, and to notify affected users where appropriate and required by law.

14.Your Privacy Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us using the details in Section 18. You can also delete individual messages yourself at any time through the in-app delete options, and stop using push notifications by disabling them in your device/browser settings.

15.Children's Privacy

Cute is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly allow children under the minimum age required by applicable local law to create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, remove it.

16.International Users

If you use Cute from outside the country where our servers are hosted, your information will be processed and stored in that hosting location, which may have different data protection laws than your own country.

17.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service evolves — for example, as retention automation or encryption features described above are completed. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and material changes will be reflected here.

18.Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be directed to the Cute development team via the contact details on our GitHub profile.
